## 1. Submitting a Copyright or Illegal Content Takedown Notice

If you believe that content hosted on **xaynxai.com** (including user-deployed sites under `/s/:slug` or public showcase items) infringes your copyright, trademark, or statutory rights, please send a written notice to **[contact@xaynx.com](mailto:contact@xaynx.com)** containing:

1. Identification of the copyrighted work, trademark, or right claimed to have been infringed.
2. The exact URL(s) on XaynxAI where the allegedly infringing material is located.
3. Your full legal name, organization (if applicable), email address, and phone number.
4. A statement that you have a **good-faith belief** that the disputed use is not authorized by the rights owner, its agent, or the law.
5. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the rights owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.
6. Your physical or electronic signature.

## 2. Review & Removal Process

Upon receiving a complete and valid notice, XaynxAI promptly investigates and, where appropriate, removes or disables access to the reported content and notifies the responsible user. Repeat infringers are subject to permanent account termination.

## 3. Submitting a Counter-Notice (Appeal)

If your content was removed by mistake or misidentification, you may submit a written counter-notice to **[contact@xaynx.com](mailto:contact@xaynx.com)** including:
1. Identification of the removed material and its prior URL.
2. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed due to mistake or misidentification.
3. Your full legal name, contact details, and electronic signature.
