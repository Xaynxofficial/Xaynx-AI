**Last Updated:** November 19, 2026

These guidelines govern the proper reference and protection of the **Xaynx / XaynxAI** name, official emblem, and custom iconography owned exclusively by **Xaynx Company**.

## 1. Official Brand Naming

- **Correct:** `XaynxAI`, `Xaynx AI`, `Xaynx Company`
- **Incorrect:** `xaynx.ai` (unless officially announced), `XaynxGPT`, `Official Xaynx Partner` (without written authorization)

- The official **XaynxAI emblem** (fluid blue, purple, and turquoise intersecting curves) and the **Custom Xaynx SVG Icon Set** are proprietary copyrighted works of Xaynx Company.
- Press, journalists, and reviewers may refer to `XaynxAI` in editorial coverage provided the official emblem is not altered, distorted, recolored, cropped, or used to imply endorsement.

---

## 3. Strictly Prohibited Brand Uses

Without prior written permission from Xaynx Company, you may **not**:
1. Copy, extract, or reuse the **Custom Xaynx SVG Icon Set** in third-party websites, templates, apps, or icon libraries.
2. Modify the XaynxAI logo's proportions, colors, gradients, or combine it with another brand's mark.
3. Register domain names, social media handles, mobile apps, or browser extensions containing `Xaynx` or `XaynxAI` in a way that suggests official affiliation.
4. Use XaynxAI branding in connection with unlawful, misleading, or harmful products.
