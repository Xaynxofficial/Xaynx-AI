# Privacy policy

This Privacy and Data Security Policy ("Policy") outlines the data processing principles, confidentiality rules, storage, and disposal standards for personal and enterprise data processed through digital platforms, autonomous AI assistants, coding environments, and APIs managed by Xaynx AI Teknoloji ("Company", "we", "us", or "our").

**Independence Notice:** Xaynx AI is an independent platform and has no direct partnership or sponsorship affiliation with OpenAI, ChatGPT, Anthropic, or Google. All referenced trademarks belong to their respective owners.

---

## Personal Data We Collect

When you visit, use, or interact with our Services, we collect the following categories of information regarding your activity:

- **Account & Identity Information:** We collect your name, email address, secure credentials, and session identifiers when creating an account.
- **User Content:** We collect prompt inputs, uploaded documents, code snippets, and generated outputs provided through our Services.
- **Log Data:** We automatically collect information sent by your browser or device, including your IP address, browser type and configuration, request timestamps, and interaction logs.
- **Usage Data:** We process information regarding how you navigate and interact with our Services, including features used and interaction activity.
- **Device Information:** We collect operating system, technical identifiers, and device attributes to enforce platform integrity.
- **Realtime Audio & Video Stream:** Realtime audio, camera, or screen streams are never processed for biometric identification. They reside in volatile RAM only during live inference and are discarded immediately thereafter.

## Google User Data and Limited Use Disclosure

Our application strictly uses user data received from Google accounts (name, email, profile picture) solely to authenticate user identity and execute core application functions. This data is never sold, leased, or transferred for marketing or advertising purposes.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

**Google Workspace & AI Models:** Xaynx AI does not use data obtained from Google Workspace APIs to develop, improve, or train generalized AI/ML models.

**Other Third-Party OAuth Extensions (GitHub, LinkedIn, Slack, Notion, Spotify, Canva, Epic Games):** All third-party integrations connected via /extensions are enabled strictly with your explicit OAuth authorization, tokens are stored securely, and data is accessed only to execute your requested actions. You may revoke any integration at any time with a single click from the Extensions page.

**Autonomous Agents & Scheduled Background Execution:** Active scheduled autonomous agents you configure may execute automatically on our cloud infrastructure (Cloudflare Workers) according to your schedule—even while you are offline—and deliver their generated reports into your linked chat. You may pause or permanently delete any agent at any time.

## Retention & Automated Disposal

We will retain your Personal Data only for as long as we need it to provide our Services to you, or for other legitimate operational purposes such as resolving disputes, safety, security, and statutory compliance:

- **Information we keep until you delete it:** Our Services allow you to delete Personal Data stored in your account, such as chat histories or coding workspaces, which are purged from active systems within 30 days unless longer retention is legally required.
- **Information we delete automatically:** Certain live streaming inputs (audio/camera via WebRTC) reside strictly in volatile RAM for realtime inference and are automatically wiped upon session completion.
- **Information retained for legitimate safety, security, or legal requirements:** In certain situations, we may retain Personal Data even after an account deletion request to satisfy regulatory mandates or prevent fraudulent conduct. For example: - If an account is banned for violating our terms of service, we may retain audit logs to prevent evasion and protect platform safety. - If required by lawful subpoena or judicial process, we retain records for the duration prescribed by law. - When you complete a transaction or subscription, we retain fiscal and invoice metadata to fulfill tax and accounting standards. - When you submit a data erasure request, we retain an immutable audit log confirming compliance with your request.

Retention and Disposal Schedule by Category:

| Data Category | Retention Period | Disposal Method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chat & Prompt Records | Until deleted by user or account active | Instant cryptographic purge |
| API & MCP Keys | Until revoked by user | Immediate de-authorization & deletion |
| Live Camera & Audio Stream | Transient inference only (in RAM) | Zero retention / instant RAM wipe |
| System & Connection Logs | Statutory regulatory duration | Periodic automated overwrite |
