# AI Safety & Transparency Statement — XaynxAI

**Last Updated:** November 19, 2026

XaynxAI is committed to transparent, safe, and human-centric artificial intelligence aligned with **EU AI Act Article 50** transparency obligations and international safety best practices.

---

## 1. AI Interaction Transparency (EU AI Act Art. 50)

- Users are always clearly informed that they are interacting with an artificial intelligence system when using XaynxAI chat, voice, vision, code, CAD, or autonomous agent tools.
- Outputs generated by AI models—including text, code, 3D/CAD files, presentations, videos, and images—are machine-generated and should be labeled or verified appropriately when shared or published.

---

## 2. No Training on Private User Data

- **Private Chats & Code:** XaynxAI does not use private user conversations, uploaded files, or workspace code to train public foundation AI models without explicit opt-in consent.
- **Google Workspace & OAuth Data:** Data retrieved via Google Workspace APIs (Gmail, Drive, Calendar) or other user-authorized OAuth extensions is strictly excluded from generalized AI/ML model training.
- **Ephemeral Voice & Camera Streams:** Live voice and camera feeds are processed in ephemeral memory for real-time inference only and are never permanently stored or archived.

---

## 3. Accuracy, Hallucinations & High-Stakes Verification

AI models are probabilistic and may occasionally produce inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated outputs ("hallucinations"). Accordingly:
- **No Professional Advice:** Outputs do not constitute legal, medical, financial, or certified engineering advice.
- **3D / CAD Engineering Caution:** Parametric 3D models and STEP/STL/DXF technical drawings generated via the CAD engine are for drafting and prototyping only and must be validated by a qualified engineer prior to physical manufacturing.
- **Code & Security Caution:** Generated software code should be reviewed and tested before production deployment.

---

## 4. Safety Guardrails & Abuse Prevention

- XaynxAI enforces automated safety filters against CSAM, malware/phishing deployment, self-harm promotion, and prompt-injection abuse.
- Deployed websites are automatically scanned for malicious signatures before publication.

For AI safety concerns or feedback, contact **[contact@xaynx.com](mailto:contact@xaynx.com)**.
