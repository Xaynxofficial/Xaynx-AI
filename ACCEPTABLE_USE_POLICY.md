# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) — XaynxAI

**Last Updated:** November 19, 2026  
**Applies to:** All users, visitors, workspaces, deployed sites, autonomous agents, and API consumers of **xaynxai.com**.

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines the boundaries of safe, lawful, and responsible use of **XaynxAI**. Violation of this policy may result in immediate content removal, feature restriction, or permanent account termination.

---

## 1. Strictly Prohibited Activities

You may **not** use XaynxAI to create, host, automate, or distribute:

1. **Illegal Content & Exploitation:** Any content involving child sexual abuse material (CSAM), child exploitation, human trafficking, terrorism, violent extremism, or illegal narcotics/weapons trade.
2. **Cyberattacks & Malware:** Viruses, ransomware, spyware, phishing pages, credential harvesters, exploit kits, DDoS scripts, or unauthorized intrusion tools.
3. **Fraud & Deceptive Impersonation:** Fake banking/government portals, financial scams, non-consensual intimate imagery, or malicious deepfakes designed to defraud or harass individuals.
4. **Safety & System Bypass:** Attempting to jailbreak safety guardrails, execute prompt-injection attacks against platform infrastructure, scrape/harvest platform data with automated bots, or circumvent rate limits and fair-use quotas.
5. **Intellectual Property Infringement:** Hosting pirated software, infringing copyrighted media, or misappropriating third-party trademarks via deployed sites or showcase items.
6. **Unauthorized Resource Abuse:** Running cryptocurrency miners, proxy/VPN relays, spam mailers, or abusive high-load background loops on XaynxAI hosting or autonomous agent infrastructure.

---

## 2. Responsible Use of Specialized Features

- **Autonomous Agents:** Scheduled agents must only automate lawful tasks and must not spam external endpoints, harass third parties, or violate third-party terms of service.
- **Website Deployment (`/s/:slug` & Custom Domains):** All published sites are also governed by our [Site Publishing Rules](./policies/SITE_PUBLISHING_RULES.md).
- **3D / CAD & Code Generation:** Generated code and parametric CAD models (STEP/STL/DXF) must be reviewed by a qualified person prior to production deployment or physical manufacturing.
- **Live Camera / Voice Modes:** You must obtain consent before capturing or streaming third-party faces, voices, or confidential materials.

---

## 3. Enforcement & Reporting Violations

XaynxAI reserves the right to investigate suspected violations, suspend accounts, remove non-compliant deployed sites, and cooperate with competent legal authorities where required by law.

To report abuse or a violation of this policy, contact **[contact@xaynx.com](mailto:contact@xaynx.com)** with the relevant URL and description.
